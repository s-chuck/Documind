## DocuMind — Problem Statement = 12 Aug 2026

People regularly work with large collections of documents such as study notes, workplace documentation, policies, manuals, insurance documents, and reports. Finding specific information across these documents can be time-consuming, especially when the answer requires understanding information from multiple sections or documents.

Existing AI tools can answer questions about uploaded documents, but the interaction is often temporary and users may have limited control over organizing their documents into a persistent knowledge base. Users also need to be able to verify where an AI-generated answer came from rather than relying on an unsupported response.

DocuMind aims to provide a persistent, personal document knowledge platform where users can store their documents, ask natural-language questions across one or multiple documents, and receive answers supported by citations pointing back to the relevant source content.

The initial version will focus on individual users who regularly work with documents and want to make finding, understanding, and connecting information across those documents faster and easier.
